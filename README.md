## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Python to analyze employee attrition trends and uncover actionable workforce insights.

The dashboard enables HR teams and analysts to:
- Monitor employee attrition  
- Identify high-risk segments  
- Analyze salary, age, and job role impact  
- Make data-driven retention decisions  

---

- Streamlit

- Plotly

## 🧠 Business Insights (Key Findings)

### 🔴 High Attrition Drivers
- Certain departments show **higher attrition rates**, indicating workload or management issues  
- Specific job roles are more prone to attrition  

### 💰 Salary Impact
- Lower salary employees tend to have **higher attrition probability**  
- Compensation plays a key role in retention  

### 👥 Demographic Trends
- Attrition varies across gender and age groups  
- Younger employees show **higher turnover rates**  

### ⚠️ Risk Identification
- High-risk employees can be identified early  
- Helps HR take **proactive retention actions**
